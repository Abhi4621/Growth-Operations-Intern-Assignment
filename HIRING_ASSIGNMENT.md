# TuteDude — Operations Intern Take-Home Task

**Company:** TuteDude  
**Role:** Operations Intern  
**Estimated Time:** 45 to 60 minutes

## Welcome to the TuteDude Operations Task!

Operations at a fast-growing startup is about staying organized, helping the team run smoothly, following up on leads, and making sure scheduled meetings actually happen.

We designed this task to test simple, everyday intern skills:
1. Basic percentage math & spotting obvious issues.
2. Finding real companies and writing a polite, short message.
3. Organizing your 2 hours of daily working time.
4. Writing a clear weekly summary for your manager.

> **Authenticity Note:**  
> Please use real companies and real LinkedIn profiles. Generic answers copied directly from AI tools (like using fake names or writing long corporate buzzword essays) will be disqualified. Keep your answers brief, honest, and practical.

---

## Task 1: Basic Pipeline Math & Spotting Problems (15 mins)
*Tests: Basic Math & Practical Problem-Solving*

Here are the numbers from our outreach last month:

| Stage | What Happened | Number |
| :---: | :--- | :---: |
| 1 | Potential leads found online | 100 |
| 2 | Leads we contacted | 80 |
| 3 | People who replied | 20 |
| 4 | People who booked a meeting | 8 |
| 5 | People who actually joined the call | **3** *(5 missed!)* |
| 6 | People who wanted to move forward | 2 |

### Your Answers:
1. **The Numbers:**
   - Reply Rate = $20 / 80 = \mathbf{25\%}$
   - Meeting Booking Rate = $8 / 80 = \mathbf{10\%}$
   - Show-Up Rate = $3 / 8 = \mathbf{37.5\%}$
   - Interest Rate = $2 / 3 = \mathbf{66.7\%}$
2. **Where is the biggest problem?**  
   In 2 sentences: What is going wrong in this process?
3. **How would you fix it?**  
   Name 2 simple, practical things an intern can do (e.g. sending a WhatsApp reminder, confirming the meeting link) to make sure more people show up for their booked calls.

## Task 2: Finding 2 Real Companies & Writing Outreach (20 mins)
*Tests: Online Research & Communication*

Pick **2 real companies** you know or find online (e.g. Razorpay, Urban Company, Swiggy, Meesho, Zerodha, etc.).

### For each company, provide:
1. **Company Name & Website URL**
2. **Person to Contact:** Name, their role (e.g. Founder, Ops Lead, HR), and their LinkedIn link.
3. **Short Outreach Message:**  
   Write a polite, 3 to 4 sentence message asking for 5 minutes of their time to explore how TuteDude interns can help their operations. (Keep it under 75 words).

---

## Task 3: Your 2-Hour Daily Intern Schedule (15 mins)
*Tests: Time Management & Organization*

As an intern, you will work **2 hours per day** (Monday to Friday, 10 hours total for the week).

Pick **2 main areas** to focus on:
- Searching for new leads online
- Sending introductory outreach emails
- Following up with leads who have not replied yet
- Sending reminders for scheduled calls

### Your Deliverable:
Write down what you will do during your 2 hours each day (Monday to Friday):
- **Monday (120 mins):** What specific task will you do? (e.g. Find 15 leads on LinkedIn)
- **Tuesday (120 mins):** What specific task will you do? (e.g. Send 15 emails)
- **Wednesday (120 mins):** What specific task will you do? (e.g. Follow up on older leads)
- **Thursday (120 mins):** What specific task will you do? (e.g. Remind tomorrow's booked calls)
- **Friday (120 mins):** What specific task will you do? (e.g. Clean the spreadsheet & send weekly update)

## Task 4: Friday Weekly Update for Your Manager (10 mins)
*Tests: Clear Communication*

At the end of every week, interns send a quick 3-bullet update to their team lead on Slack or email.

Write your weekly update answering:
1. **The Biggest Blocker:** 1 sentence on what was hard or slowed you down this week.
2. **3 Tasks Finished:** 3 bullet points of what you completed.
3. **3 Priorities for Next Week:** 3 bullet points of what you will work on next.
